Summary: Stepura has no user accounts and no servers of our own. Your steps, workouts, streaks and challenges are stored on your device. Two features need a network connection: maps (provided by Mapbox) and subscription purchases (handled by Google Play). Both are explained below.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Stepura ("we", "our", "us"). Stepura is a mobile fitness application that counts your daily steps, runs step challenges and streaks, records GPS walks and runs, and lets you progress through virtual walking expeditions.

By using Stepura, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. No Account, No Sign-Up

  • Stepura does NOT require an account, email address or login
  • We have NO servers or databases that store your personal data
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT use advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • We cannot access, view or retrieve the data the app stores on your device

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the app's functionality, Stepura stores the following information on your device only, using the device's internal storage:

3.1 Profile Information

  • Name (for personalized greetings)
  • Age and gender (for accurate calorie calculations)
  • Weight and height (for stride length and calorie burn calculations)

3.2 Fitness & Activity Data

  • Daily step count (from the device's motion sensor or from Health Connect)
  • Estimated calories burned, distance walked and active time
  • Daily step history

3.3 Workout & Expedition Data

  • Recorded GPS routes, distance, duration and pace for walks and runs you choose to record
  • Progress through virtual expeditions and earned badges

3.4 Challenge & Progress Data

  • Active and completed challenges, and daily challenge completions
  • Streak counts and history
  • Milestone achievements

3.5 App Preferences

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Notification settings
  • Daily step goal
  • Whether a Stepura Pro subscription is active (an entitlement flag only — no payment details)
Important: All data listed above is stored on your device. We do not upload it, and we cannot access, view or retrieve it.

4. Location Data (GPS Workouts)

If you choose to record a walk or run, Stepura uses your device's precise location to draw your route on a map and calculate distance and pace.

  • Location is used only while a workout is being recorded. The app does not request or use background location outside of an active, user-started recording.
  • Recording runs in a foreground service with a visible notification, so it is always clear when tracking is active. You can pause or finish at any time.
  • Your recorded routes are saved on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • You can decline or revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app continues to work.

5. Health Connect

Stepura can read your daily step count from Android Health Connect, so that step goals, streaks and challenges reflect steps already recorded by another app or a wearable.

  • Access is read-only and limited to the Steps data type (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS). No other health data type is requested.
  • Step data read from Health Connect stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or shared with third parties.
  • This connection is optional and can be revoked at any time in Health Connect settings.
  • Health Connect data is never used for advertising, and is never sold or transferred.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Mapbox (maps)

Maps used in GPS workouts and expeditions are provided by Mapbox. To display maps, the app requests map tiles over the internet, and the Mapbox SDK may send location and usage telemetry to Mapbox in accordance with their privacy policy. This means location data may be shared with Mapbox as a third party when you use map features.

See the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

6.2 Google Play Billing (subscriptions)

Stepura Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. All payments are processed by Google — we never see, receive or store your card or payment details. The app stores only a flag indicating whether a subscription is currently active, so paid features work offline.

See the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Google Play account.

8. Data Security

  • Your app data is stored on your device and protected by your device's built-in security (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • The network requests the app does make (map tiles and Google Play Billing) use encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Because we keep no copy of your data, there is no server-side database of yours that could be breached

9. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Clear app data: Go to your device Settings → Apps → Stepura → Storage → Clear Data. This permanently deletes all app data.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling Stepura automatically removes all stored data from your device.

Since we have no servers or backups, once data is deleted from your device it cannot be recovered by us or anyone else. For data held by Mapbox or Google Play, please refer to their privacy policies linked above.

10. Children's Privacy

Stepura is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
