1. Introduction

Welcome to Stepura ("we", "our", "us"). Stepura is a mobile fitness application that helps users track daily steps, create fitness challenges, maintain activity streaks, and monitor health-related metrics. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our application.

By using Stepura, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Stepura is designed with your privacy as a priority. We want to be transparent:

  • We do NOT collect any personal information
  • We do NOT transmit any data to external servers
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT use advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • We do NOT share any data with third parties
  • We do NOT require user accounts or login
  • We have NO servers or databases that store your data

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the app's functionality, Stepura stores the following information locally on your device only using the device's internal storage (AsyncStorage):

3.1 Profile Information

  • Name (for personalized greetings)
  • Email (optional, for future features)
  • Age and gender (for accurate calorie calculations)
  • Weight and height (for stride length and calorie burn calculations)

3.2 Fitness & Activity Data

  • Daily step count (from device motion sensors)
  • Estimated calories burned
  • Estimated distance walked
  • Active time duration
  • Daily step history (last 30 days)

3.3 Challenge & Progress Data

  • Active and completed challenges
  • Daily challenge completions
  • Streak counts and history
  • Milestone achievements

3.4 App Preferences

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Notification settings
  • Daily step goal
Important: All data listed above is stored exclusively on your device. It never leaves your phone. We cannot access, view, or retrieve this data in any way.

4. Device Permissions

Stepura requests the following Android permissions:

  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — Required to access the device's step counter sensor. This is the core functionality of the app. Without this permission, step counting will not work.
  • INTERNET — Required by the React Native framework. Stepura does not make any network requests or transmit any data.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Allows the step counter to properly reset its baseline when the device restarts, ensuring accurate daily step counts.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • No data is transmitted over the internet, eliminating the risk of interception
  • No cloud storage means no risk of server breaches

6. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Clear app data: Go to your device Settings → Apps → Stepura → Storage → Clear Data. This permanently deletes all app data.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling Stepura automatically removes all stored data from your device.

Since we have no servers or backups, once data is deleted from your device, it cannot be recovered by anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

Stepura is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is intended for users aged 18 and above.

8. Third-Party Services

Stepura does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media SDKs. The app operates entirely independently and offline.
